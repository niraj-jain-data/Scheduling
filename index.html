This week's focus — Copilot Agent, hands-on

Tue, Wed and Thu carry a dedicated 60–minute sprint to build one working Copilot agent inside your actual PBWM workflow — start with a single repetitive task (a data refresh check, a report summary, or a QA pass on a dashboard) and get it running end to end before adding a second use case. Treat the Hands-On LLMs reading blocks as the theory that feeds this build, not a separate track.

On Mon/Tue/Thu, meditation and planning now happen at the office after the gym (8:50–9:15), not at home — a quiet corner or an empty meeting room works if your desk doesn't. Work starts a little later (9:15) on those days to account for it. A 20-minute power nap sits right after lunch on those same days, using the 1 CP collaboration area, before the Copilot sprint — this is the classic early-afternoon dip, so it's the highest-value place to spend it. The evening free blocks on Mon, Tue, Wed and Sat now carry two clear halves — leisure reading (rotate "The Word Detective" and "A Little History of Everything") and chatting with friends — so both have a real home instead of competing with each other or with the phone. Every weekday afternoon now gives up an hour of deep work to AI study (Mon/Tue/Thu: a second Copilot-adjacent build hour; Wed: the AI Engineer block runs to 2 hours; Fri: the old "wrap up tasks" hour is now a full study block) — and Saturday's study block runs 30 minutes longer. The Mon/Tue/Thu commutes (2 hrs/day total) are now pleasure reading or spiritual audio — read if you've got a seat, listen if you're standing. The walk to each spiritual session (not the walk home, which stays as quiet reflection) is now spiritual audio too. Sunday's Weekly Big Review sits at 1:00–2:30 PM, ahead of the evening session. Every deep-work block now opens with an environment cue before the priorities — library-quiet corner plus a whiteboard warm-up to structure the day's BI tasks — since that's where your flow state actually shows up. The thinking-walk blocks now open with a purpose check-in instead of a generic prompt, since that's what you've told me those walks are actually for. Mon, Tue and Wed now open the evening with a 20-minute Work Reflection walk right after work ends, before commute/gym — everything after it shifts 20 minutes later, including bedtime (10:00 → 10:20 PM on those three nights). Thu and Fri already had this covered: the walk home from the spiritual session now explicitly prompts a work reflection too, alongside the spiritual one, so nothing new was added there. Wed's evening was also quietly broken before this (the home workout started at 5:30, half an hour before work actually ended) — that's fixed now too.
